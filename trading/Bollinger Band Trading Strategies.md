Bollinger Band Trading Strategies
1. Bollinger Band Bounce Strategy
Buy Setup
Price touches Lower Bollinger Band
            ↓
Market oversold
            ↓
Bullish reversal candle forms
            ↓
Buy

Example
Price = ₹980
Lower Band = ₹975

Hammer Candle Forms

BUY

Target = Middle Band (20 EMA)

Stop Loss = Below Lower Band

Sell Setup
Price touches Upper Bollinger Band
            ↓
Market overbought
            ↓
Bearish reversal candle forms
            ↓
Sell

Example
Price = ₹1050
Upper Band = ₹1048

Shooting Star Appears

SELL

Target = Middle Band

2. Bollinger Band Breakout Strategy
Buy Setup
Volatility Expansion
         ↓
Strong Candle Closes Above Upper Band
         ↓
Volume Increase
         ↓
Buy

Example
Upper Band = 500

Price Closes = 512

Volume = 2x Average

BUY

Sell Setup
Strong Candle
        ↓
Closes Below Lower Band
        ↓
High Volume
        ↓

5. Bollinger Band Mean Reversion Strategy
Buy Setup
Price Far Below Lower Band
           ↓
RSI < 30
           ↓
Price Returns Inside Band
           ↓
Buy

Example
Lower Band = 200

Price Falls To 190

RSI = 25

Price Moves Back To 202

BUY

Common Mistakes
❌ Selling immediately at Upper Band
❌ Buying immediately at Lower Band
❌ Ignoring trend direction
❌ Trading breakouts without volume
❌ Ignoring VWAP confirmation
❌ Trading during low-liquidity periods
