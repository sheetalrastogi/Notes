## 2. RSI, VWAP and Volume Trading strategies
- VWAP identifies institutional direction, 
- RSI measures momentum, and 
- Volume confirms conviction behind the move.

## Combined RSI + VWAP + Volume setup

**Bullish Setup**
```text
Price Above VWAP  +  RSI Between 50-70   +  Volume Above Average
        ↓
Bullish Pullback
        ↓
Buy
```

**Example**
```text
Price = 1020
VWAP = 1005

RSI = 58

Volume = 1.8x Average

Pullback To VWAP

Bullish Candle

BUY
```

**Bearish Setup**
```text
Price Below VWAP  +    RSI Below 45   +   High Selling Volume
        ↓
Retest VWAP
        ↓
Sell
```
