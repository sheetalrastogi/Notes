## Executive Summary

These QA Efficiency Metrics provide end-to-end visibility into:

- Testing Speed
- Team Productivity
- Automation Effectiveness
- Quality Outcomes
- Cost Optimization
- Resource Utilization
- DevOps Maturity
- AI Adoption & Productivity

Together, they help Test Managers, QE Architects, QA Directors, and GCC Leaders measure and continuously improve the business value delivered by the Quality Engineering organization.
